# Website screenshots

The website uses real Ditero development captures in English, with fictional household data. The source is the MIT-licensed [Ditero application](https://github.com/iuliandita/ditero). Captures are served as WebP in matching light/dark pairs, with full-size image links and localized alternative text.

The captures come from the published v0.1.0-beta.4 images. Every displayed excerpt has native 1x and 2x files; nothing is upscaled or resampled. The desktop dashboard excerpt uses a native 438x518 crop from a 980x800 capture, showing the complete priorities card at its Large size preset. Matching full-size 2x originals remain linked.

The development screenshots illustrate current source capabilities. Published beta downloads contain a subset. Do not label these captures as a released beta or imply that the mobile capture is a separately qualified native installation.

The wordmark and app icon come from Ditero's original brand assets. Schibsted Grotesk and Noto Sans Arabic are bundled from their Fontsource packages under their included SIL Open Font Licenses.
